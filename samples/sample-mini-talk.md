# Mini Talk — How to Speak So People Listen

> A short demo note for the Shadowing Workshop. Replace the video link below with your own,
> then open this note in the workshop to practice shadowing with synced subtitles.
> Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eIho2S0ZahI

[00:00] We all speak. But do we speak in a way that others want to listen?
[00:07] There are habits that make our voices powerful, and habits that drain them away.
[00:15] The first habit is honesty — speak what you truly mean.
[00:22] The second is authenticity — stand in your own words.
[00:29] The third is integrity — be the person you claim to be.
[00:36] And the fourth is love — wishing the listener well as you speak.
[00:44] Warm up your voice. Move it through your chest, your head, and your nose.
[00:52] Use prosody — let your pitch rise and fall with meaning, like a melody.
[00:59] Slow down for what matters, and let silence do some of the heavy lifting.
[01:07] If you speak with honesty, authenticity, integrity, and love,
[01:14] the world will sound a little more beautiful.
